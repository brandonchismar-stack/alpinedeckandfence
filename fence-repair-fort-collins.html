What Goes Into a Lasting Wood Fence Repair?

Repairing the visible damage is only part of the job. The surrounding wood and support posts determine whether a smaller repair will hold up.

1. Find Out Why the Fence Failed

For a leaning fence, we check for rot where the post meets the ground, a loose or damaged footing, separated rails, and neighboring posts that may also be moving. Resetting a panel without addressing a failed post may not solve the problem.

2. Save Sound Wood When Practical

If rails and cedar pickets are reusable, replacing the affected post or rails may be more sensible than removing the entire fence. For a damaged gate, we check alignment, the support post, hinges, latch and framing before recommending hardware work or a rebuild.

3. Match the Repair to the Damage

A single-post repair, loose picket replacement, gate adjustment and storm-damaged section rebuild are different scopes. We explain what needs to come out, what can remain and whether access, concrete removal or debris disposal affects the estimate.

What Affects Fence Repair Cost?

The number of failed posts, the condition of surrounding wood, access to the fence and whether a gate needs hardware or rebuilding all affect the scope. Reattaching sound rails is different from replacing rotted posts and rebuilding a section.

For example, a repair involving one failed post and reusable rails is a different scope from a post with a large concrete footing to remove, or several sections with broken framing. A sagging gate may need a hinge or latch adjustment, a new support post, or a new frame depending on the cause.

Common Questions About Fence Repair in Fort Collins

Not sure whether your fence needs a small repair or a rebuilt section? Here is how we approach the most common requests.

Can you replace just one broken fence post?

Often, yes. If the surrounding rails and pickets are usable, we can assess replacing the damaged post and reconnecting the existing fence section instead of quoting an entire new fence.

Can a wind-damaged fence section be repaired?

It depends on the posts, rails, and overall condition of the wood. We can discuss a targeted repair or replacement of the damaged section based on what will provide a sound result.

Do I need exact measurements before calling?

No. Tell us your location, whether the issue involves posts, panels, or a gate, and roughly what happened. Photos and approximate measurements help, but they are not required to start an estimate.

Before Replacing a Fence Post: Underground Utilities

Post replacement may require digging near existing utilities. The excavator must arrange a Colorado 811 public utility locate before excavation. Private lines, including some sprinkler and irrigation lines, are not covered by the public locate and must be identified separately.

Let us know about nearby sprinkler heads, irrigation pipes, landscape lighting or other private lines when discussing your repair. This helps plan the work and avoid unnecessary surprises.
