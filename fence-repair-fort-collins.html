Fence Problems We Repair

Many damaged fences do not need a full tear-out. The repair depends on whether the posts, framing, pickets, and nearby sections can still support a sound result.

Broken or Leaning Fence Posts

A rotted, snapped, or loose post can make the entire section lean. We assess the post and surrounding rails, then quote the necessary post replacement and reattachment work.

  • Damaged wood post replacement
  • Leaning privacy fence sections
  • Rail and picket reattachment

Wind & Storm Damage

Colorado wind can break rails, pull pickets loose, or knock down a section of fencing. We evaluate what can be repaired and where a replacement section makes better sense.

  • Blown-down wood fence sections
  • Broken rails and missing pickets
  • Partial fence rebuilds

Gate Repair & Hardware

When a gate drags, sags, binds, or will not latch, the issue may be the hinges, framing, hardware, or supporting post. We inspect the actual cause before recommending a fix.

  • Hinge and latch replacement
  • Sagging or misaligned gates
  • Gate framing and rebuild options

Repair or Replace?

If the surrounding posts and rails are sound, a smaller repair may be enough. If several posts have failed or the structure is badly deteriorated, replacing the affected section can be the more practical option.

We'll discuss the condition, scope, and options before preparing an estimate.

How to Request a Quote

Send the address, a few clear photos, approximate fence length, and the number of damaged posts or gates. Include whether the issue followed a windstorm or developed over time.

Alpine can review the information and determine whether an on-site look is needed.
