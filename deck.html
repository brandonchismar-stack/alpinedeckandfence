Repair or replace?

The condition of the structure comes first.

Look beyond the visible damage

Deck repairs start with the condition of the framing, supports, connections, decking, stairs, and railings involved.

Use a targeted repair when it makes sense

When the remaining structure is sound, a smaller repair may address the problem without an unnecessary full rebuild.

Rebuild when the condition calls for it

If repairs won't be a sound long-term solution, Alpine can discuss partial replacement or new construction.
