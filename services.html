Services

Deck and fence services in Fort Collins.

Alpine Deck & Fence provides fence installation, fence repair, deck construction, deck repairs, and related exterior carpentry services in Fort Collins and nearby communities.

Fence Installation

New privacy fence installation, replacement sections, full tear-out and rebuild scopes, and gate framing.

  • Cedar privacy fencing
  • Post, rail, and picket replacement
  • Single and double gates

Fence Repair

Storm damage, leaning sections, blown-out runs, loose gates, and targeted repair work where full replacement is not necessary.

  • Wind damage repair
  • Hardware and hinge replacement
  • Selective rebuild work

Deck Work

New decks, framing, repair work, decking replacement, and exterior carpentry scopes tied to existing structures.

  • Deck framing
  • Deck repairs
  • Trim and finish carpentry
