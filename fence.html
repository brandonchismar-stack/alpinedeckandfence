The repair decision

Repair what can be saved. Rebuild what cannot.

Start with the failed post or connection

Look for movement at the footing, wood deterioration, loose rails, or hardware that no longer holds alignment.

Keep usable materials when practical

If surrounding rails and pickets are sound, a targeted repair may be more sensible than replacing the entire fence.

Match the replacement to the damage

One broken post, a sagging gate, and multiple wind-damaged sections require different repair scopes.

Small repair requests are welcome. We assess the actual conditions before preparing an estimate.

Before you request an estimate

Tell us what you're dealing with

A rough fence length, the number of posts or gates affected, and a few pictures can help—but exact measurements are not required to make contact. Alpine serves Fort Collins and nearby Northern Colorado communities, including Loveland, Windsor, and Timnath.

Can one damaged post be replaced?

Often, yes. If the surrounding fence is sound, we can discuss replacing the post and reconnecting the existing section.

What if wind knocked down part of a fence?

We assess the posts, rails, and pickets to determine whether a repair or replacement section makes better sense.

Have a fence project?

Share the damage or describe the new fence. You'll speak directly with the owner.
